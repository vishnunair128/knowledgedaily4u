Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Knowledge Daily (“we”, “us”) runs knowledgedaily4u.online. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. By using our website you agree to it.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email, phone number and message when you contact us or buy a plan.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Health helper chat: questions you type into the chat are used to answer you. If the AI assistant is switched on, they are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write a reply, without your name or email. Please do not share sensitive personal details in the chat.
  • Suggestions: if you send a suggestion through our feedback form (hosted by Google Forms), we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Sign-in details: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Health inputs: the age, height, weight, food, workout, water and habit logs, and the daily check-in answers (how you feel and any symptoms you share), that you enter in our tools are stored in your own browser. If you sign in, they are also saved to your account in Google Firebase so they can sync across your devices and power your performance page. If you do not sign in, we do not receive them.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and secure.

How we use it

To deliver what you bought, process payments and refunds, reply to your messages, send service updates, prevent fraud and meet our legal duties.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with Razorpay Software Private Limited to process payments, with Google LLC (Google Sign-In and Firebase) to sign you in and store your synced data, with other service providers who help us run the website, and with authorities when the law requires it. Google may process data on servers outside India.

Cookies and local storage

Our tools use your browser’s local storage to remember your settings and logs. You can clear this any time in your browser settings.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw your consent. Write to us at vishnunair128@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can ask us to delete your account data at any time, and we will delete it from our database.

Children

Our services are meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
