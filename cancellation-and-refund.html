Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy applies to purchases made on knowledgedaily4u.online from Knowledge Daily.

Cancellation

Our Plus plans (1 month, 3 months and 1 year) are one-time payments for a fixed period. They do not renew automatically, so no further charges are made unless you buy again. To cancel an order, email vishnunair128@gmail.com before you start using the plan.

Refunds

Because our products are digital, they cannot be returned. We will refund your payment if:

  • you ask within 7 days of purchase and have not substantially used the product,
  • you were charged twice for the same order, or
  • a technical problem on our side stops you from accessing what you bought and we cannot fix it.

How to request a refund

Email vishnunair128@gmail.com with your name, the email or phone used at checkout and your Razorpay payment ID. We will review your request and reply within 3 business days.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it.

Failed payments

If money was deducted but your order was not confirmed, the amount is normally returned automatically by your bank within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not, write to us and we will help.

Not refundable

Requests made after 7 days, or for products that have been fully used or downloaded, are not eligible except as required by law.
