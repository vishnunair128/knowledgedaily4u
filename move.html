Try a 20-minute routine with no equipment

Do each move for 40 seconds with 20 seconds of rest, and repeat the circuit 3 times: squats, push-ups (on your knees if needed), glute bridges, plank, reverse lunges, and jumping jacks or marching on the spot. Warm up with 2 minutes of easy walking and stretch at the end.
